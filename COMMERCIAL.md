## Controlled Design-Partner Pilot

The controlled design-partner pilot is a scoped technical engagement:

- Participation: by invitation after workflow-fit review
- Duration: four to six weeks
- Scope: one workflow, one authoritative evidence model, and one frozen rule bundle
- Output: versioned adapter or snapshot contract, fixed rules, acceptance fixtures,
  canonical JSON receipts, replay package, unsigned actions where applicable,
  acceptance report, and production-readiness gaps

Commercial terms and any case-study permission are agreed privately. The
signed order form controls the named engagement.

The pilot excludes production funds, custody, signing, transaction submission,
insurance, underwriting, legal or regulatory advice, production SLA, and a
mainnet-readiness claim.

Templates and acceptance controls are kept in Ward's private development
repository, not in this public repository.
